# How to add your roadmap

Everyone in GDG on Campus TUM keeps a personal roadmap here. It takes about 10 minutes.

## Steps
1. Make sure you have accepted your invitation to the **GDG-TUM** organization.
2. Go to the **Issues** tab of this repository and click **New issue**.
3. Choose **Member Roadmap** and fill in the form.
4. Submit it. The core team will add it to the **Chapter Roadmap** project board.
5. Tick your milestones off and add comments as you progress.

## Tips
- Keep your plan realistic and specific. "Learn cloud" is vague. "Deploy one app on Cloud Run by December" is a plan.
- Link your repos and certificates in the issue as you finish them.
- Update your issue at least once a month. Monthly check-ins go through everyone's progress.
- Ask for help in the "Help you need" section. That is what the chapter is for.

## Make your membership public
On the organization's **People** page, find your name and change the visibility to **Public**, so your profile shows you are part of GDG on Campus TUM.
